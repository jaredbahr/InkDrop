# InkDrop 0.1.0-alpha.41

- Manual Search checks ranked Prowlarr child indexers under bounded deadlines, continuing past rejected noise until useful evidence is found.
- Exact safe SLSKD results can move forward automatically instead of always showing `Manual retrieval required`.
- Automatic and Manual Search now share trusted collected-edition aliases and unit evidence across providers.
- Related titles, wrong units, multiple volumes, and malformed release-group suffixes remain blocked.
- Prowlarr search work is capped at eight ranked child lanes with per-indexer and overall deadlines, avoiding slow aggregate searches and unbounded late workers.
